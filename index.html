What's in the bottle

AFIA is purified drinking water with a measured mineral blend added back for a soft, rounded taste. It is not spring or natural mineral water, and we label it honestly.

1 · Source

Deep borehole water, permitted by the Lagos State Water Regulatory Commission and tested every quarter.

2 · Purify

Sand and carbon filters, then reverse osmosis removes impurities, metals and salts.

3 · Balance

Calcium, magnesium and potassium are added back in fixed amounts, for taste and a pH around 7.6.

4 · Protect

Ozone and UV sterilisation, then sealed in heavy, recyclable PET in a NAFDAC-standard plant.

Questions

Is AFIA registered with NAFDAC?

Every AFIA product carries its NAFDAC registration number on the label. Registration numbers are shown here once issued.

How do I pay?

At launch: bank transfer, or card and transfer through our secure payment page sent with your order confirmation. Payment on delivery is available for repeat customers.

Can I return bottles?

Unopened, undamaged cases can be returned within 48 hours of delivery. Damaged or short deliveries are replaced free.

Do you deliver outside Lagos?

Ogun State (Ota, Mowe, Ibafo) for orders of 20 cases or more. Ask us on WhatsApp for other states.

Is the bottle recyclable?

Yes. All AFIA bottles are 100% recyclable PET, and wholesale customers can return empties for collection.
